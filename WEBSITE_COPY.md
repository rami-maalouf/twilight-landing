## Privacy Policy

### Our Philosophy
Twilight is built on a simple principle: **your data is yours**. We don't collect it, we don't store it on our servers, and we certainly don't sell it. Everything stays on your device.

### What We Don't Collect
- Sleep data or patterns
- Health information
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Personal information

Seriously—we have no servers storing your data. The app works entirely offline.

### Local Storage Only
All your sleep logs, goals, and preferences are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, your data goes with it. We have no way to recover it because we never had access to it in the first place.

### Android Waitlist
If you join our Android waitlist, we collect only your email address to notify you when the Android version launches. This email is stored securely and will never be shared with third parties.

### Open Source
Twilight is open source. You can inspect every line of code to verify our privacy claims. Transparency isn't just a buzzword for us—it's the foundation of trust.

## Terms of Service

### The Short Version
Twilight is a simple sleep tracking app. Use it to build better sleep habits. Don't use it for anything weird. That's basically it.

### What Twilight Is
Twilight is a sleep tracking tool designed to help you maintain a consistent sleep schedule. It tracks three things: when you sleep, when you wake up, and how consistent you are. That's it. No complex metrics, no confusing data, no subscriptions.

### What Twilight Isn't
- A medical device or diagnostic tool
- A replacement for professional medical advice
- A treatment for sleep disorders like insomnia
- A wearable or hardware product

If you have serious sleep issues, please consult a healthcare professional. Twilight is built for the average person who wants to maintain better sleep habits.

### Your Data
All your data stays on your device. We don't have access to it, we can't see it, and we definitely can't sell it.

### Open Source
Twilight is open source software. You're free to inspect, modify, and contribute to the codebase. We believe in transparency and community-driven development.

### No Guarantees
We can't guarantee Twilight will fix your sleep. What we can guarantee is that we've built a tool focused on what actually matters—consistency—without the bloat, subscriptions, or data harvesting of typical sleep apps.
